H.264, 30 fps, YUV 4:2:0, BT.709, MP4 fast-start; no audio, location metadata or auxiliary camera streams. Landscape: 1280 × 720. Portrait: 608 × 1080, keeping the full camera frame (even dimensions for encoding). The workshop source uses HDR; its derivative is tone-mapped to SDR for consistent browser playback. WebP posters come directly from the corresponding clips.

The gallery loads a clip only after the visitor presses play. Do not add autoplay, preload the clips or replace the original footage with generated installation imagery. Labels describe the visible work, without assigning unverified client names or technical certifications. Update `src/data/installation-films.ts` when replacing an asset, including its dimensions and duration.
